# Product Brief

## Who it serves

Learners and evaluators comparing public procurement-negotiation mechanisms under transparent stress conditions.

## Decision

Decide whether a non-oracle mechanism is reliable enough to recommend across the complete demand-volatility, capacity, and supplier-risk grid. A recommendation requires convergence and transfer feasibility in every cell. Allocation-capacity feasibility remains visible as separate evidence because it can still invalidate a practical result.

## Output

The headless command writes a deterministic JSONL evidence set and Markdown rollup with an all-cells qualification recommendation.
