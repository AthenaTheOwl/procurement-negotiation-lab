# cost-benefit transfer

The operational plan and the surplus split are separate.

First, agents coordinate on a quantity. Then the transfer engine computes
whether the global surplus is large enough to make both sides no worse off than
their outside options.

If surplus is negative, the app does not force a happy story. It reports that no
transfer can make every party whole under the modeled terms.

Positive buyer transfer means supplier pays buyer. Negative buyer transfer means
buyer pays supplier. Transfers sum to zero.
