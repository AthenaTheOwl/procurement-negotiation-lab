# Level 06 — A third party

## Recap
**Last level:** Mechanism choice trades welfare, privacy, and speed.
Two parties only.

## Stakes line
A packager joins. Now there's a chokepoint, and the surplus has to
split across all three.

## Setup
The two figures become a cytoscape graph (reuse the existing
`SourceGraph` component from spec 0008):
- 3 nodes: buyer, supplier, packager.
- 2 edges: buyer → supplier (demand) and supplier → packager
  (substrate).

Below the graph:
- A `<QuantityKnob>` for the packager's capacity.
- A `<SplitRuleToggle>` with 3 stops: `proportional`, `equal`,
  `shapley`.
- A `<TransferLedgerVisual>` showing arrows of transfer amounts along
  the graph edges.

## Interaction
The user:
1. Drags the packager's capacity slider down. Watch the buyer's
   achievable q drop. (Demonstrates chokepoint.)
2. Toggles the split rule. Watch the transfer-arrow widths change.
   (Demonstrates fairness rules.)

## Reveal
A 3-row table appears showing, per party: utility before transfer,
outside option, transfer amount, utility after. All three rows show
green checkmarks (no party walks) under `shapley`; under `equal`, one
party might be undercompensated.

One sentence: **"Three parties share the surplus differently
depending on the rule. Shapley splits by each party's marginal
contribution; equal splits ignore it; proportional sits between."**

## Continue
Enabled after the user has tried at least 2 of the 3 split rules.

## Components used
