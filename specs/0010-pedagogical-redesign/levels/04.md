# Level 04 — Splitting the surplus

## Recap
**Last level:** With cost-band sharing, the joint surplus is $Y.
Someone has to decide how to split it.

## Stakes line
A deal needs both parties above their walkaway. Find a split where
both stay in the room.

## Setup
Two `<UtilityCurve>` panels side by side, one per party:
- **Buyer curve**: utility vs share. Horizontal dashed line shows the
  buyer's outside option ($8,400 default from `data/agents.ts`).
- **Supplier curve**: same shape, with supplier outside option ($5,200).
- A single shared **share slider** (0%-100%) between the curves.

Each curve has a **deal zone** (green tint, above outside option) and
a **walkaway zone** (red tint, below outside option).

## Interaction
The user drags the share slider. Both curves' active markers move
together; the shared (combined) outcome is shown as two pins on the
curves. Live label: **"Both above outside option ✓"** or
**"Supplier would walk ✗"**.

## Reveal
When the user finds a share where both pins land in their green zones
for >500ms, the two figures swap to `mood="happy"` and a Lottie
`nod-yes` plays. One sentence:

**"Both above the walkaway line. The transfer is feasible — surplus
gets split, the deal closes."**

If the user releases in a walkaway zone, the failing party's figure
plays `walk-away.json` once.

## Continue
Enabled once the user has reached a feasible split at least once.

## Components used
- `<UtilityCurve party="buyer" outsideOption={8400} />`
- `<UtilityCurve party="supplier" outsideOption={5200} />`
- `<DealZone />` (helper for green/red shading)
- `<QuantityKnob>` repurposed as the share slider
- `<AgentFigure>` (mood transitions)
